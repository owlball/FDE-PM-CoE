About FDE

What is Forward Deployed Engineering?

FDE embeds SAP engineers and PMs directly with strategic customers to build production-grade AI agents — scoped, validated, and handed off in one motion.

🔍

Discovery (W0–W6)

Six weeks with the customer to identify the highest-value AI use case, validate technical feasibility, build a proof of concept, and produce a signed spec and Go/No-Go decision.

⚙️

Execution (90 days)

Sprints-based build of the approved agent on SAP BTP. Jira backlog, sprint ceremonies, staged environments, and a clear path to Release to Customer (RTC) and GA.

Discovery Process

From alignment to handoff

From SAP internal alignment to handing off a signed spec to the Execute team. Full detail at the Discovery Guide & Templates site.

00

W0 · Pre-work

Internal Alignment

Sweep account knowledge, resource the SAP squad, confirm FBA scope. Get the team briefed before the customer sees anything.

01

W0 · Onboard

Onboard Both Teams

Name the full team on both sides, sign the 6-week plan, confirm FTEA is in motion. No system access until FTEA is back.

02

W1 · Generate

Build the Use Case Longlist

Map the customer landscape, present FDE AI use cases, collect the customer's own backlog. Check all candidates against SAP's AI roadmap in Neo.

03

W1–W5 · Define

Define, Build & Validate

L6 process decomposition, AS-IS & TO-BE flows, thin-slice backend POC on real customer data, draft spec and mock UI, feasibility and value assessments.

04

W5–W6 · Prioritize

Go/No-Go Decision

Converge to one use case, score all four criteria, 30-min SAP executive decision meeting, executive sign-off on spec. A single Go must emerge.

05

W6 · Pre-Execute

Hand Off to Execute

Define scope across four dimensions, name the Execute team on both sides, build the 90-day plan. Discovery Lead stays until the first Execute session runs.

Execution Phase

From signed spec to live product

The 90-day build phase. Jira-based sprints, a mixed SAP/client dev team, and one PM wearing three hats.

⚙

The Role

Product Owner · PMO · Scrum Master

You own the delivery framework end-to-end. Product Owner for the backlog, PMO for governance and reporting, Scrum Master for cadence. Expect travel for key ceremonies. Single point of accountability from kick-off to production.

🔄

The Cadence

Two-Week Sprints on Jira

90-day MVP cycle. Two-week sprints with planning, standups, demos, and retrospectives. You set the pace, maintain the backlog, and remove blockers so the dev team can build.

📋

Deliverables

Plans, Roadmaps & Reporting

Sprint plans and summaries, development and deployment roadmaps, and status reporting calibrated to three audiences: SAP management, FDE sponsors, and the client. Documentation is a first-class deliverable, not an afterthought.
